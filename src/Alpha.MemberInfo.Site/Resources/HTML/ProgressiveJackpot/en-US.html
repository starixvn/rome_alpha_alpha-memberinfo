Introduction

Progressive Baccarat Jackpot allows player to place a side bet that has chance to take home the offered progressive jackpot. The jackpot amount is displayed on the top of the screen.

Jackpot Contribution

Contribution Amount

The size of the Jackpot pool is based on the contribution made from certain % of total bets on the side bet that contributed by SMA & house, depends on the Position Taking of each party.

The contribution amount = {Position Taking %} * {contribution rate} * {Jackpot bet value}

  • Jackpot bet value: the fixed amount required when placing a bet on the Jackpot game.
  • Jackpot pool: the pool that is used to pay for the Progressive Jackpot prize.
  • Contribution rate: the percentage of each bet goes into the Jackpot pool.

Example:

  • The Position Taking on member = 10%
  • Total contribution rate = 5%
  • Jackpot bet value = $0.25

Note: the contribution amount will be rounded to 4 decimal places.

→ The contribution amount = 10% * 5% * $0.25 = $0.00125 ≈ $0.0013

Payout Condition

Type Responsible Payout
Mega Jackpot House
  • Over MYR 200,000: contact with Customer Service Department to claim the winning.
  • MYR 200,000 or lower: winning amount will be credited to member’s balance.
  • The jackpot bet amount will NOT be refunded.
Other prizes House & SMA
  • The winning amount will be credited to member’s balance.
  • The jackpot bet amount will be refunded (included in the payout).
